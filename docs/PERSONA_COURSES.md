# Persona courses (탐색 탭)

NVIDIA [Nemotron-Personas-Korea](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-Personas-Korea) (synthetic Korean people matched to real population statistics) played one by one by Gemini: each persona decides who they go with and where, then writes a timetable from sights and dining spots around that area. Generated by `scripts/fetch-personas.mjs` → `select-personas.mjs` → `build-persona-courses.mjs` → `retitle-persona-courses.mjs`. The courses the 탐색 tab had before are kept in `src/features/mobile/sample-courses.curated-backup.json`.

The people are synthetic; nothing here is about a real person.
